Dealing With Leaking Pipe Water Damage in Corpus Christi

You seldom catch a pipe leak the day it begins. It drips inside a wall, under a sink or below the slab, and the first thing you notice is a stain, a soft spot in the floor or a water bill that makes no sense. Often that means weeks of moisture have already gone into whatever surrounds the pipe. We serve Corpus Christi homeowners by tracing the full wet area and drying every bit of it, including what is out of sight.

A plumber can stop the drip, though the water that soaked into the structure is still there afterward. That is our part of the job. We use moisture meters and thermal imaging to map the wet materials, open only what has to be opened, and set air movers and dehumidifiers until readings are back to normal. A slow leak gives mold plenty of time to begin, so we inspect for growth too and treat any surface it has reached.

First Five Minutes

Before We Arrive

  1. Shut Off the WaterWhen a toilet or sink line is dripping, shut the valve on the supply right at that fixture. When the source is unclear, the house main is the valve to turn off.
  2. Keep Power AwayWater around a switch, outlet or light fixture means the breaker for that part of the house should go off. Leave any wet cord or electrical device alone.
  3. Read the MeterWith every tap off, look at the meter. If the dial is still moving, a pipe is leaking somewhere you cannot see, possibly under the slab.
  4. Empty and PhotographClear out the cabinet or closet near the leak and take photos of stains, warped materials and the pipe itself before anything is repaired or thrown away.
  5. Get a Moisture CheckBefore the opening is sealed, get the surrounding materials checked with a moisture meter. A wall that feels dry on the surface can still be wet inside the cavity.
Will Insurance Pay?

Does Insurance Cover It?

Insurers draw a line between a sudden leak and a slow one. Coverage is common for accidental, sudden damage, while a leak that ran for months or weeks is usually not covered and is specifically excluded in some policies. If the leak was concealed and you could not have known, it may be viewed more favorably than a visible leak nobody dealt with. Read your policy and talk to your agent. We document the source of the leak, moisture readings and photos so the adjuster has clear facts.

Why It Happens

What Causes It

  • Pinhole corrosion in older copper lines that slowly eats through the pipe wall.
  • Failed solder joints or crimp rings hidden inside wall and ceiling cavities.
  • A P-trap or drain connection under a sink that loosens and drips each time the basin empties.
  • A hairline split left by freezing weather in a line running through a crawl space or outside wall.
  • Soil movement and rubbing that wear a hole in a line buried below the slab.
What Is at Risk

Where the Damage Spreads

Flooring Damage

Water from a slab or wall leak travels under flooring. Laminate seams swell, hardwood boards cup, and the glue holding tile or vinyl releases.

Hidden Mold Growth

Nothing suits mold better than the dark, moist space inside a wall. It can spread across the hidden face of the drywall long before anything appears on the room side, which is common when a leak is slow.

Stained Drywall and Ceilings

Leaks over a ceiling or behind a wall surface show as bubbling paint and brown stains. Drywall that stays wet goes soft, sags and may need to be cut out.

Wet Insulation

Insulation in the wall soaks up leak water like a sponge and holds it against the framing. Since a closed wall gives it almost no airflow, soaked insulation is normally removed.

Our Process

How We Handle Leaking Pipe Water Damage

1

Map the Moisture

Using a thermal camera and moisture meters, our crew checks cabinets, floors and walls and marks each wet spot, even those far from the pipe that water reached by running along the framing.

2

Make Targeted Openings

To get at wet cavities we open only specific spots, take out unsalvageable cabinet parts, drywall or insulation, and seal anything moldy in bags so its spores stay out of the rest of the home.

3

Structural Drying

We keep dehumidifiers and air movers going until the subfloor and framing read normal, which usually takes 3 to 5 days. We check readings on each visit and adjust the equipment as materials dry.

4

Treat and Document

Affected surfaces get an antimicrobial treatment, and you receive photos along with the moisture logs. That paperwork proves the area was dry ahead of repairs and can support a claim.
